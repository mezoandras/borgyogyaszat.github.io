Welcome to Dr. Judit Krizsa's private dermatology clinic - where the latest methods meet personal attention.

Our aim is to provide dermatological care to the highest professional standard, using the most modern methods and tools. Our clinic is located at Szent István tér 13 in the heart of Szeged city centre and is easily accessible by public transport and by car - with an accessible entrance.

Arranging an appointment is simple: patients are seen at the reception desk, by telephone, or after booking online, so they can receive the care they need as soon and as smoothly as possible.

Our work always centres on our patients' needs and expectations. In addition to medical examinations, we also provide cosmetic treatments and advice when requested. We hope you will have a pleasant experience with us.

Frequently Asked
Questions

Can I pay by bank card?

Yes, you can also pay by bank card at our clinic, including by PayPass (contactless payment).

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

Yes, booking in advance is required. You can book online, by telephone, or at the reception desk.

Who performs the examinations?

Examinations are personally performed by Dr. Judit Krizsa, dermatologist.

Can you issue an invoice to a business?

We can issue invoices only to private individuals. Unfortunately, we cannot issue invoices to businesses or under a health fund number.

How can I get to the clinic?

The clinic is at Szent István tér 13 in Szeged city centre. It is easily accessible by both public transport and car. The entrance is accessible.

Where can I find the notices?

The privacy notice is available on the GDPR page.

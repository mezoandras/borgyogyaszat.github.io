2. Scope of This Notice

This notice applies to use of the borgyogyasz.at website, contact by email and telephone, and online appointment booking available through the Minup system.

The clinic will provide information during care on the detailed rules governing the processing of medical records created in the course of healthcare and related data processing.

3. Data Processed and Purposes of Processing

Website Use

Technical data, such as IP address, browser type, page opened, and the time of the visit, may be generated in connection with the operation of web hosting and the browser. This data is processed on the basis of the legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of the website.

Contacting Us

When you contact us by email or telephone, your name, contact details, and information provided in your enquiry are processed to answer your question, arrange an appointment, and take steps prior to a possible contract. The legal basis for processing is taking steps at the data subject's request prior to entering into a contract and the clinic's legitimate interest.

Online Appointment Booking

The online booking interface operates through the Minup system. Data provided during booking, in particular the name, email address, telephone number, selected service, and time, are necessary to arrange the appointment and stay in contact with the data subject. The legal basis for processing is a step taken at the data subject's request prior to entering into a contract and performance of the resulting healthcare relationship.

Please provide only data necessary for the booking in the notes field of the booking interface. Health data are special categories of personal data and may be processed only to the extent necessary to provide healthcare.

4. Data Retention

Enquiries and booking data are retained only for the period necessary to make contact, arrange the appointment, and manage possible legal claims. The retention of documentation created in the course of healthcare is governed by the applicable healthcare and accounting legislation.

5. Processors and Data Transfers

The data controller may use service providers for operation of the website, online booking, and technical operation. Data may be accessed only by persons and service providers who require access to perform their duties.

  • The website's hosting and technical operator may process the technical data necessary to make the website available.
  • Minup is the technical provider of the online appointment booking interface; processing performed on this interface may also be subject to Minup's own notice.
  • Where required by law or in response to an authority's request, the data controller is entitled or obliged to transfer the necessary data.

6. Cookies and External Services

The website source code does not contain its own analytics or marketing tracking code. The online appointment booking interface may use technical cookies or similar technologies for its operation. Further information is available in the relevant provider's privacy notice.

7. Data Subject Rights

You have, in particular, the following rights in relation to data processing:

  • to request information about and access to your processed personal data;
  • to request rectification of inaccurate personal data;
  • where necessary, to request erasure of data or restriction of their processing;
  • to object to processing where its legal basis is legitimate interest;
  • to request data portability in cases specified by law;
  • to seek remedy before the supervisory authority or the courts.

The data controller will respond to your request without undue delay and, as a general rule, no later than one month.

8. Complaints and Legal Remedies

If you believe that the processing of your personal data violates applicable data-protection rules, you may lodge a complaint with the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH): 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11; mailing address: 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9; email: ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu; website: www.naih.hu.

9. Changes to the Privacy Notice

The data controller may amend this notice where necessary. The current version is always available on this page together with the effective date of the amendment.
